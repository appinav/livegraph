How the System Works

Step 1: The user enters the number of rooms and people.

Step 2: The user enters the city to obtain current weather and forecast information.

Step 3: Household equipment is added with its power rating and daily usage.

Step 4: The weather analysis estimates whether fan and AC usage may increase.

Step 5: The weather-adjusted energy estimate is calculated.

Step 6: The monitoring graph simulates household load and voltage changes.

Prototype limitation: This system does not directly measure mains voltage, current, power or electricity consumption. The monitoring values are calculated estimates for demonstration. A real electrical monitoring system would require suitable electrical sensors and an appropriate measurement circuit.

Privacy Policy

This prototype is designed to minimize data collection. The website does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information.

Information entered by the user

The user may enter a city name, household configuration, equipment information, power values and usage times. These values are used by the website to perform the requested calculations and weather analysis.

Weather information

When the weather feature is used, the entered city name is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to find the corresponding geographical coordinates. The coordinates are then used to request weather information from Open-Meteo.
