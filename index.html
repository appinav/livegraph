How the Simulation Works

The estimated household load is based on the number of rooms, number of people, and the electrical equipment configured above.

Each equipment item contributes its configured power consumption. Daily energy is estimated from power and daily usage time.

The voltage displayed by this prototype stays around a nominal household voltage and changes slightly as simulated load changes.

These values are estimates generated by software. They should not be used for electrical safety decisions, utility billing, equipment certification, or professional electrical engineering.
